# India Travel Insurance: A Service Design Audit

**Live deck:** https://apoorva-sdm.github.io/india-travel-insurance-analysis/

This project was part of the Service Design course in the M.Des Strategic Design Management programme at NID Gandhinagar (2026).

## What is service design?

Service design looks at a service as a whole. That means not just the app or website a customer sees, but also the people, processes, policies and handoffs behind it. It asks how all of these work together, where they break down, and what that costs the customer and the business.

A service design audit follows a real service from start to finish. It maps each point where the customer comes into contact with the company and finds the gaps between what the service promises and what people actually experience.

## Why travel insurance?

Many organisations fall short on the service they provide. They focus on what is visible and overlook the intangible parts of the experience, or they misunderstand the core need of the people they serve.

Insurance is a clear example. The core need it should meet is assurance: knowing you are safe if something goes wrong. Our view is that, for many insurers, this has become a secondary concern and profit has become the core one. For customers, this shows up as claims that are not paid, long and draining claim processes, and conditions buried in the fine print.

Our survey supported this. We received 51 responses. Between them, respondents had travelled to 74 destinations, and only 6 had received a payout.

## Why HDFC ERGO?

None of our survey respondents had used HDFC ERGO, and the app reviews we scraped for it did not give us useful insights. With so little to go on from customers, we chose it for an in-depth study and service audit.

We also compared it against four other insurers to see which problems were specific to one company and which were common across the category.

## What we did

- A survey with 51 respondents
- Field interviews with bank insurance specialists and underwriters
- About 2,150 app store reviews across five insurers, analysed for sentiment, emotion and recurring keywords
- A service audit of HDFC ERGO, including a service safari and user archetypes

## How it was built

The deck was built through vibe coding with Claude. We described what each section needed to show, then reviewed and refined the code over many rounds. It uses Three.js for the 3D elements and GSAP for the scroll animations, and everything is in a single HTML file.

The review analysis was done in Python, using a transformer model for sentiment and YAKE for keyword extraction.
